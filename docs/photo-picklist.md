# Photo Pick-List — House of Happiness

A checklist for replacing the 9 current Wikimedia attraction photos with nicer, licence-free
ones, plus 4 new photos for upcoming articles.

## How to use this list

1. Open the search URL for a slug (all links below are clickable).
2. Pick a **landscape** shot you like — it is used as a wide hero/card image.
3. Download the **largest** size offered.
4. Resize to **~1600px wide** (height whatever it falls out at), export JPEG quality ~80.
5. Aim for **under ~400 KB** per file. (Current `iconsiam.jpg` is 596 KB and `watpho.jpg` is 583 KB — both too heavy.)
6. Save as exactly `<slug>.jpg` — **lowercase, no spaces** — into:
   `/home/user/houseofhapiness/images/attractions/`
7. Overwrite the old file with the same name. Every page that uses it updates automatically. Nothing else to edit.

### What to avoid
- Big recognisable faces in the foreground (portrait-style tourist shots).
- Shots dominated by a brand logo or a sign for a specific restaurant/shop.
- Vertical / square crops — they get cropped badly in the wide hero slot.
- Anything smaller than ~1600px on the long edge.

### Honesty note on the links below
Where it says **VERIFIED CANDIDATE**, that exact photo page appeared in a search result and
the URL is real. Where it says **SEARCH LINK**, it is a search/collection page — open it and
pick from what you see. No photo IDs or direct file URLs have been invented.

# Second-choice source: official TAT media library

## What to do after you have replaced the photos

- [ ] Every slug you replaced with an Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay photo → **delete its entry from `credits.json`**.
- [ ] Every slug you left on Wikimedia (likely `kudichin` and `skypark`) → **leave its entry exactly as it is**. The credit must stay visible.
- [ ] If you replace all 9, `credits.json` becomes an empty array `[]` and the credits box can be removed from the page entirely.
- [ ] If you shoot any photo yourself, no entry is needed — you own it.
- [ ] The 4 new slugs (`boatguide`, `gettingaround`, `ayutthaya`, `songkran`) need **no** credits entries at all, as long as you take them from Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay as recommended.

**Best realistic outcome:** 7 of 9 replaced with no-attribution photos, `kudichin` and
`skypark` shot on your own phone on a walk, and `credits.json` emptied completely.
