House of Happiness sits in Khlong San, only ~2 km from ICONSIAM — 5 minutes by car or one BTS Gold Line stop. Pay less than a third of riverside hotel prices and enjoy the same neighbourhood.

Getting to ICONSIAM from our hotel

  • BTS Gold Line (cheapest): walk or motorbike-taxi to Khlong San station → ride one stop to Charoen Nakhon — the exit connects straight into ICONSIAM. ~15 minutes total.
  • Taxi / Grab (easiest): ~5-10 minutes, around ฿50-70 — perfect when you're carrying shopping bags.
  • Walk (scenic): ~25-30 minutes through real Khlong San neighbourhoods and local markets on the Thonburi side.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get back late at night from ICONSIAM?

Easily — taxis/Grab take 5-10 minutes, and the BTS Gold Line runs until around midnight. Self check-in is available if you tell us your arrival time.

Can I walk to ICONSIAM?

Yes, ~25-30 minutes along Somdet Chao Phraya and Charoen Nakhon roads — or take the Gold Line one stop from Khlong San station.

How far ahead should I book?

1-2 weeks ahead for holidays. Direct bookings through our site are confirmed within 24 hours — ask for rates on LINE/WhatsApp.
