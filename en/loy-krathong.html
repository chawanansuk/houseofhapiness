Loy Krathong 2026 on the Chao Phraya — Thonburi side

🗓 Wed 25 Nov 2026 🌕 Full-moon night 🏮 Floating spots within walking distance
Illustration · Wikimedia Commons · credits

Loy Krathong falls on Wednesday 25 November 2026 (the full moon of the 12th lunar month). The Khlong San riverside is one of Bangkok's loveliest places to float a krathong — right on the water, with lights on both banks.

Where to float near our hotel (Khlong San / Thonburi)

  • Khlong San riverside piers: several piers and waterfront spots in our neighbourhood open for floating on the night — walkable from the hotel, authentic local atmosphere, far less crowded than the old-town side.
  • ICONSIAM river park (Charoen Nakhon): in recent years it has hosted the biggest riverside Loy Krathong event on the Thonburi side — floating decks, shows and markets. One BTS Gold Line stop from Khlong San.
  • Chao Phraya Sky Park + Memorial Bridge: not for floating, but the prettiest full-moon viewpoint — krathong lights shimmering down the whole river, ~15 minutes' walk away.
  • Riverside temples: several Thonburi temples open their piers with temple fairs on the night. Ask us at check-in which ones are hosting this year — we're locals.

Tips for the night

  • Timing: peak crowds are 19:00-21:00. Go before 18:30 for photos, or float after 21:30 when it's calmer but still magical.
  • Choose a biodegradable krathong: banana trunk, leaves or bread — kind to the river you're making wishes on. Vendors at every venue sell them.
  • Book early: riverside rooms sell out fast every year. Stay with us and walk to the river — no bridge traffic at 9 pm.

Frequently asked questions

When is Loy Krathong 2026?

The night of Wednesday 25 November 2026 (12th lunar month full moon). Check each venue's announcement near the date — many run events across several days.

Where's the nearest floating spot from the hotel?

Khlong San piers are walkable, and ICONSIAM's big event is one Gold Line stop away — you can walk home and skip festival-night traffic entirely.

How far ahead should I book?

At least 3-4 weeks ahead — riverside rooms fill fast. Book direct (2-night minimum), confirmed within 24 hours.
