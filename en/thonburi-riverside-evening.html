Half Day · Thonburi riverside

Riverside Cafés, the Sky Park & Kudi Chin — a Thonburi Evening

🕓 Late afternoon to evening · ~3–4 hours 🚶 On foot + a short ride · no river crossing 🌇 Time the Sky Park for the golden hour 📍 Every stop is on the Thonburi bank near us
Illustration · Wikimedia Commons · credits

Some days you don't need to cross the river at all — our Thonburi bank lines the water with good things already. Leave the hotel in the late afternoon, take a riverside café on the Chao Phraya, walk up to the Sky Park as the light turns gold, then wander into Kudi Chin, the old riverside quarter with Santa Cruz Church and charcoal-baked cakes. This is the easy half-day evening we love to suggest when guests want to slow down.

Why this evening stays on one bank

Most guests overlook that our Thonburi bank has its own handsome stretch of river. The Chao Phraya Sky Park — the world's first public park built across a river bridge — is only about 1.3 km from the hotel, or a ~15-minute walk, and Kudi Chin, the 200-year-old riverside quarter, sits on the same side just past the bridge. No ferry to catch, no long ride. So we shaped this gentle evening to amble between them, catch the light, and be back in bed within minutes.

The route

  1. 4:00 PM — Leave the hotel · a riverside café in Khlong San: start slow with an iced coffee at a café on the Chao Phraya around Khlong San and ICONSIAM, catching the river breeze before you walk. Ask us at check-in which spots are open and have a good view at that time of year.
  2. 4:45 PM — Walk the riverbank towards Memorial Bridge: follow the water from Khlong San towards Memorial Bridge (Saphan Phut), past piers and old Thonburi riverside houses. Take it slowly and photograph the river as the light softens. (Lhong 1919, which used to sit on this stretch, has closed.)
  3. 5:30 PM — Chao Phraya Sky Park at golden hour: walk on (about 1.3 km from the hotel) up to the Chao Phraya Sky Park, the world's first park on a river bridge. Stroll out over the middle of the river for a 360° view — arrive around sunset for the finest light.
  4. 6:30 PM — Dusk in Kudi Chin: come down off the Sky Park on the Thonburi side and walk into the riverside Kudi Chin quarter. Stop by Santa Cruz Church, try the charcoal-baked khanom farang cakes made to the old recipe, and find a quiet riverside dinner in the old neighbourhood.
  5. 7:30 PM — Home: from Kudi Chin a Grab or taxi back to Khlong San takes ~10 minutes (or retrace the riverbank on foot if you still have the legs). Shower and sleep — a full evening that never wore you out.

Small tips from us

  • Timing the light: aim to be on the Sky Park about 30 minutes before sunset — you catch both the golden light and the city lights coming on.
  • Shoes and water: this half day is mostly walking, ~3–4 km. Wear comfortable shoes and carry water; the evening river breeze on the Thonburi side is just cool enough.
  • Dress for the sacred spots: Santa Cruz Church and Wat Kalayanamit in Kudi Chin are living places of worship — cover shoulders and knees, keep voices low, and ask before photographing interiors.
  • Rainy-season swap: if showers threaten, wait it out at the riverside café, then head up to the Sky Park once the sky clears — or ask us to check the forecast at check-in.

Common questions

Do I need to take a ferry or a long ride?

Not at all — every stop is on the same Thonburi bank as the hotel. The Sky Park is only about 1.3 km away, walkable. Coming back from Kudi Chin you can take a short ~10-minute Grab if you're tired.

How late is the Sky Park open — can I go in the evening?

The Sky Park stays open into the evening, though closing time can shift by season. Go around sunset to be safe, and check the current closing time beforehand — or just ask us at check-in.

Is Lhong 1919 still open?

It has closed. This route now follows the riverbank up to the Sky Park and on to Kudi Chin instead. If you want old Chinese shophouses by the river, try Song Wat Road across the water — a ฿5 ferry from the pier at the end of our lane (we have a separate guide).
