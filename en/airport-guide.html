Getting Here · Khlong San, Bangkok

Getting here from the airport

🚕 BKK 45–60 min · ~฿350–450 🚕 DMK 40–60 min · ~฿250–350 🚆 Train ~฿100 · to Khlong San station

House of Happiness is in Khlong San on the Thonburi side of the Chao Phraya River — reachable from both Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang. Here's every option with real fares and times.

📋 Address to show your driver (tap to copy)

เฮาส์ ออฟ แฮปปิเนส (House of Happiness)
558/1 ถนนท่าดินแดง ซอย 16 แขวงคลองสาน เขตคลองสาน กรุงเทพฯ 10600

✈️ From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)

  • Metered taxi / Grab (best with luggage): ~45-60 min, ฿350-450 including tolls. The taxi rank is on Level 1 — take a queue ticket and show the driver the address above.
  • Train (about ฿100): follow the steps below, ~70-85 minutes. Best with light luggage outside rush hour.
  1. Take the Airport Rail Link from the airport basement to the last stop, Phaya Thai (~26 min, ฿45).
  2. Change to the BTS Sukhumvit Line at Phaya Thai → at Siam switch to the Silom Line → get off at Krung Thonburi.
  3. Switch to the BTS Gold Line at Krung Thonburi → ride to Khlong San, the final station.
  4. From Khlong San station it's a 10-12 minute walk or a ~2 minute motorbike taxi (฿20-30) to the hotel.

🛬 From Don Mueang Airport (DMK)

  • Metered taxi / Grab (recommended): ~40-60 min, ฿250-350 including tolls. Ask the driver to use the expressway in the evening.
  • Train: SRT Red Line to Bang Sue → MRT to Hua Lamphong → short taxi across the river. Several transfers — only worth it with very light luggage.

💡 Before you travel

  • Tell us your arrival time: staff are on site until 18:00, after that we use self check-in — message us on LINE/WhatsApp and everything will be ready.
  • Arriving before 14:00 check-in? Message us — early check-in is possible when rooms are free, or leave your bags and go explore.
  • Heading back to the airport: add +30 minutes to the usual time during 7-9 am and 4-7 pm rush hours.
