Rainy Day · an all-indoor backup, Khlong San

A rainy day still works — an all-indoor plan of malls, cafes and covered halls, staying as dry as possible

🚝 A sheltered, elevated Gold Line — one stop to ICONSIAM 🏬 A cool riverside mall — food and indoor things to do all day ☕ Plenty of cafes to wait out the heaviest rain ☂️ Borrow an umbrella from us · move between spots staying dry
Illustration · Wikimedia Commons · credits

Rain is normal in Bangkok, especially in the afternoon — but it needn't cost you the day. Our hotel in Khlong San is close to the Gold Line and a riverside mall, so today we hop from one dry spot to the next with the least exposure: a cool mall, a cafe while the rain is heaviest, and covered temple halls if it eases — then home to a room nearby without a soaking.

Why our side handles a rainy day well

On a rainy Bangkok day the problem isn't finding somewhere indoors — it's the open-air stretches between places that leave you drenched. Our Khlong San side has an edge here: the Gold Line is an elevated, sheltered track that runs one stop into the ICONSIAM area, a riverside mall you can stay inside all day, with food courts, shops and indoor attractions. So we plan to hop from one dry spot to the next — parking the heaviest rain in a cafe and saving covered outdoor sights, like the reclining Buddha hall at Wat Pho, for when it eases. And if the rain simply won't stop, staying in the mall all afternoon is a perfectly good day.

The route

  1. Check the sky, grab an umbrella from us. If you wake to rain or heavy cloud, this is the day for this plan. Borrow an umbrella from the hotel, wear shoes with grip, bag your phone and camera against the wet, and head out without rushing.
  2. One Gold Line stop to the ICONSIAM area. Khlong San station is near the hotel, the track is elevated and covered, and it's a single stop to ICONSIAM. The short walk from the station into the mall needs only a moment under the umbrella (check for a covered link on site*).
  3. Morning to midday: settle into ICONSIAM. The riverside mall is cool all day, with food courts, a wide choice of restaurants and indoor attractions to browse. Have lunch inside — no need to step out into the rain at all.
  4. Heaviest rain: park in a cafe. When it really comes down, this is the time to sit over a coffee, let the kids rest and watch the rain over the river through the glass. Wait until it lightens before moving on — don't race the weather.
  5. If it eases: ferry to a covered hall at Wat Pho. When the rain lightens, take a covered river boat across to the Phra Nakhon side. Wat Pho's reclining Buddha sits in a roofed hall you can view even under grey skies. The temple courtyards are open, so keep the umbrella; if the rain returns hard, just skip this step.
  6. Home, and dry. Ferry back or ride the Gold Line to Khlong San station and it's a short walk in. If it's pouring, a Grab can drop you at the door. Back after 6 PM is self check-in — we send the door details on LINE or WhatsApp.

Small tips from us

  • Borrow our umbrella: we have umbrellas to lend — ask at the desk on your way out, or message us if you're arriving late to self check-in.
  • Waterproof your bag: keep phone, camera and passport in a zip bag or dry pouch — Bangkok rain arrives fast and hard.
  • Grab in a downpour: if it just won't stop, a Grab or taxi to the door beats fighting through it — short hops around the area are inexpensive.
  • Cash and PromptPay: the mall takes cards, while the ferry and small shops take cash or PromptPay — keep small notes on you.
  • Ask us for today's plan: message us on LINE or WhatsApp and we'll tell you what's open in the mall this season and which spots you can reach without getting wet.

Common questions

Can we reach ICONSIAM without getting wet?

The Gold Line is elevated and covered and runs a single stop to the ICONSIAM area; the short walk from the station into the mall needs only a moment under an umbrella (check for a covered link on site*). In a heavy downpour, a Grab can drop you right at the mall.

If it rains all day, is there anything to do near the hotel?

Yes — a cool riverside mall is just one Gold Line stop away, with food, cafes, shops and indoor attractions to fill a whole day without stepping into the rain. You can happily stay put.

Can we still see a temple if it's raining?

Yes, when it eases. Wat Pho's reclining Buddha is in a roofed hall you can view even under grey skies, though the courtyards are open — so keep an umbrella. If it's really pouring, stay in the mall and save the temple for a clearer day.
