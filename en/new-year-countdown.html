New Year's Eve on the Chao Phraya — ICONSIAM Countdown Fireworks

🎆 Every 31 December 🚶 Viewing spots on foot 💬 Book direct — ask rates on LINE/WhatsApp
Illustration · Wikimedia Commons · credits

The ICONSIAM countdown is one of Asia's biggest fireworks shows — a kilometre-long display over the Chao Phraya, broadcast worldwide every year. Our hotel is on the same side of the river, close enough to walk home after midnight.

Free viewing spots on the Khlong San side

  • Khlong San / Tha Din Daeng riverside: our bank looks straight at the sky above ICONSIAM — far easier to find a spot than the mall side with its hundred-thousand-person crowds.
  • Memorial Bridge / Chao Phraya Sky Park: open river-bend views (check the park's opening announcement near the date — access can be limited on countdown night).
  • Your own balcony: city-view rooms catch the glow above the Charoen Nakhon skyline — a private celebration, no crowds.

Why stay on this side on NYE

  • Walk home: after midnight the roads jam, taxis vanish and the BTS queues run for an hour — our guests simply walk back in 10-20 minutes.
  • Far cheaper: riverside hotels near the fireworks charge 3-5× on NYE and sell out by November — staying with us costs a fraction of that (New Year rates may run slightly above standard; ask us on LINE/WhatsApp).
  • A base for the whole trip: ICONSIAM, Chinatown and the temples for New Year merit-making are all minutes away.

Our guests' countdown-night plan

  1. Evening: dinner at a local Tha Din Daeng spot (see our neighbourhood food guide) — normal prices, no festival menus.
  2. ~22:00: claim a riverside spot on foot, or take the Gold Line into the ICONSIAM event zone if you want to be in the middle of it (allow extra time back — trains are packed).
  3. Midnight: minutes of fireworks filling the river sky — our bank's camera angle rivals the mall side.
  4. After: stroll home while the mall-side crowds spend another hour hunting for taxis.

Frequently asked questions

Can you see the fireworks from the Khlong San side?

Yes — the fireworks launch over the river itself, visible from both banks. Khlong San wins on smaller crowds and the walk home.

When should I book for NYE?

Dec 30 - Jan 1 sells out faster than any other time of year — book by October-November. Direct bookings confirm within 24 hours at our direct rate (New Year dates may run slightly above standard; we confirm the price when you book).

Can I check in late on NYE?

Yes — self check-in is available any hour if you tell us your arrival time via LINE/WhatsApp when booking.
