How to use MuvMi electric tuk-tuks from Khlong San — booking, paying, where they go

🛺 Electric tuk-tuks booked in an app 💬 No haggling — price shown up front 🗺️ Parts of Thonburi, e.g. Wongwian Yai, ICONSIAM 📱 Sign-up may need a Thai mobile number
Illustration · Wikimedia Commons · credits

MuvMi runs electric tuk-tuks you book in an app. Most rides are shared with others heading the same way, with pick-up and drop-off at points the app sets, and the price is shown before you book — no haggling. It suits short hops inside its service areas; on the Thonburi side it covers some neighbourhoods, such as around Wongwian Yai and ICONSIAM. Here's how far it works for guests on our side, and when to use something else.

Check two things before relying on MuvMi

Get signed up before you head outSeveral users report that sign-up needs a Thai mobile number to receive the code. If you already have a Thai SIM or eSIM, go ahead. If not, try your own number first — and if it won't go through, use Grab or Bolt instead.
  • 1. Can you sign up?Download the app and sign up in your room on Wi-Fi — don't leave it until you're standing at the kerb.
  • 2. Is there a pick-up point near you?Service areas grow and change. Open the app to see whether the hotel, or wherever you are, is inside an area. If not, take the Gold Line to ICONSIAM or Krung Thon Buri and book from there.

How it works, step by step

  1. Download and sign up: get the MuvMi app from the App Store or Google Play; it's available in English.
  2. Add a payment method: you pay in the app; several sources say credit cards can be added.
  3. Choose pick-up and drop-off: pick from the points the app shows on the map, enter the number of passengers and check the price before confirming.
  4. Book a little ahead: cars come in rounds and may pick others up on the way, so if you're on a schedule, book early.
  5. Wait at the pick-up point: follow the car in the app, and show your booking to the driver when you board.

MuvMi also runs electric boats

Small tips from us

  • Not for big luggage: shared tuk-tuks have little room for bags — on arrival or airport days, use Grab or a taxi.
  • Rain: tuk-tuks are open-sided and you can get wet — on a heavy-rain day, take a closed car.
  • Compare prices: put the same destination into MuvMi and Grab, then choose on price and waiting time.
  • Ask us: not sure which way to go? Message us on LINE or WhatsApp and we'll help.

Common questions

Can I book MuvMi from the hotel?

It depends on whether the hotel is inside a service area at the time — the areas keep changing. Open the app to find the nearest pick-up point. If there isn't one, take the Gold Line to ICONSIAM or Krung Thon Buri and book from there, or use Grab or Bolt.

Do I need a Thai phone number?

Several users report that sign-up needs a Thai mobile number to receive the code. Try your own number first; if it fails, use a Thai SIM or eSIM, or use Grab and Bolt, which you can sign up for with your own number.

How is it different from Grab?

MuvMi is a shared electric tuk-tuk that picks up and drops off at set points inside its service areas — good for short hops. Grab is a private car to any door, better for late nights, big bags or longer trips.
