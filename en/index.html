About

House of Happiness offers serviced apartments in three styles — Standard (double bed), Studio (twin beds) and Deluxe (extra-large with sofa corner). Every unit has a city-view balcony, air conditioning, a refrigerator, and a flat-screen TV. A coin-operated laundry is available on site. Free Wi-Fi and complimentary bottled water with every stay.

Getting Here

✈️ Suvarnabhumi · Don Mueang · 🚆 BTS Gold Line — how to get here

From Suvarnabhumi Airport

Taxi ~45-60 min (~350-450 THB incl. tolls), or Airport Rail Link → BTS to Khlong San station

From Don Mueang Airport

Taxi ~40-60 min (~250-350 THB); take the expressway at rush hour

By BTS Skytrain

Silom Line to Krung Thonburi → Gold Line to Khlong San station → short walk or motorbike taxi

* Times and fares are approximate. Need help arranging a pickup? Just ask us

FAQ

What are the check-in / check-out times?

Check-in 14:00–22:00 (staff on duty until 18:00, self check-in after that — please tell us your arrival time). Check-out by 12:00 — just drop your key in the check-out box at reception.

Is there a deposit?

No key or damage deposit. You only pay for the room — cash, bank transfer or PromptPay; details come with your confirmation.

How can I pay?

We accept cash and Thai bank transfer / PromptPay. Details are provided when we confirm your booking.

How do I book directly with you?

Message us on LINE/WhatsApp or use the booking form on this site — we confirm within 24 hours. Since you talk to us personally, check-in times and special requests are easy to arrange. Ask for rates in chat.

Is there parking?

There is no on-site parking. We recommend taxi/Grab, the Gold Line (Khlong San station), or the cross-river ferry to Tha Din Daeng pier.
