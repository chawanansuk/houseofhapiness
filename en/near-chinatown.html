Craving Yaowarat street food at night without Chinatown hotel prices? House of Happiness is one bridge across the river (~2 km) — easy to go, easy to come back late, and our neighbourhood is far quieter for sleeping.

Getting to Chinatown from our hotel

  • Taxi / Grab: across Memorial or Phra Pok Klao Bridge, ~10 minutes, ฿70-100. Easy to hail on Yaowarat Road for the ride home at night.
  • Walk via Memorial Bridge (free, scenic): ~30-35 minutes past local markets and over the river to the head of Yaowarat Road.
  • Market crawl: walk to Sampeng Market (~900 m), browse the fabric and trinket lanes, and continue straight into Chinatown.

Why stay on the Khlong San side

  • Half the price: Chinatown hotels run ฿1,500-4,000 a night; ours cost far less. Three nights saves you thousands — that's a lot of street food.
  • Quiet nights: Yaowarat buzzes until 1-2 am. Eat your fill, then ride 10 minutes back to a calm riverside neighbourhood with no traffic under your window.
  • Old-town base: Wat Pho, Wat Arun and the flower market are all a similar short hop — use us as your base for the whole historic district.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get back late at night?

Taxis and Grab are available on Yaowarat Road all night — ~10 minutes back. Self check-in means you can return as late as you like (just tell us your arrival time).

Which nights are best for Yaowarat street food?

Evenings every day except Monday, when many stalls close for street cleaning. Tuesday-Sunday gives you the full spread. For a detailed eating route, see our 2-hour Yaowarat night food walk.
